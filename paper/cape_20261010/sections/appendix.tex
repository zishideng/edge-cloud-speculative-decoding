\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}
All numbers, tables and figures in this paper are generated from retained raw records by \path{scripts/make_assets.py}; the text reads every value from the generated \path{numbers.tex}. The raw records, power traces, frozen plans and source snapshots are in \path{results/rerun_20261028}, one directory per session:
\begin{itemize}
\item main comparison: \path{gemma3_main}, \path{llama_main}, \path{qwen_main} and \path{gemma2_main} (all nine methods of a pair interleaved in one session);
\item delay sweep (Gemma-3, 10/25/50/200\,ms): \path{gemma3_sweep}; its 2 and 100\,ms points use the same 30 prompts of \path{gemma3_main};
\item ablation and energy-weight sweep (Gemma-3): \path{gemma3_ablation} and \path{gemma3_weights}.
\end{itemize}
Models: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\cite{llama3}, Qwen2.5-0.5B/7B-Instruct~\cite{qwen25}, Gemma-2-2B/9B-it~\cite{gemma2} and Gemma-3-270M/4B-it~\cite{gemma3}, with the quantizations of Table~\ref{tab:pairs} (AWQ~\cite{awq2024} for the Llama, Qwen and Gemma-2 targets).
