\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}
All numbers, tables and figures in this paper are generated from retained raw records by \path{scripts/extract_evidence.py} and \path{scripts/make_assets.py}; the text reads every value from the generated \path{numbers.tex}. The raw records, power traces, frozen plans and source snapshots are in the repository:
\begin{itemize}
\item Llama: \path{results/final_20261020/llama_full_dist_110} (Sync-4, fixed windows, \cape{}) and \path{results/baselines_20261022/llama_new_110} (PEARL, PicoSpec, PipeSD, HF assisted);
\item Qwen: \path{results/final_20261020/qwen_full_dist_110} and \path{results/baselines_20261022/qwen_new_110};
\item Gemma-2: \path{results/final_20261020/gemma_full_dist_110} and \path{results/baselines_20261022/gemma_new_110};
\item Gemma-3: \path{results/gemma3_20261026/gemma3_full_110} (all ten methods), \path{gemma3_delay_sweep} and \path{gemma3_ablation};
\item PipeSD threshold tuning: \path{results/baselines_20261022/*_pipesd_tune_v2} and \path{results/gemma3_20261026/gemma3_pipesd_tune}.
\end{itemize}
Requests of the same pair, condition and prompt are paired across phases; all phases of a pair share the prompts, seeds, dynamic delay schedules, draft server and verification service. Models: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\cite{llama3}, Qwen2.5-0.5B/7B-Instruct~\cite{qwen25}, Gemma-2-2B/9B-it~\cite{gemma2} and Gemma-3-270M/4B-it~\cite{gemma3}, with the quantizations of Table~\ref{tab:pairs} (AWQ~\cite{awq2024} for the Llama, Qwen and Gemma-2 targets).
